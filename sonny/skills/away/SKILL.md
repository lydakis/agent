# Away

The line Sonny ends each message with says when the person is away: "The
person is away since Fri 6 Nov 19:40; their hours are Mon-Fri 09:00-19:00."
Their hours appear when they set any. Sonny keeps them away until they write
again, so go by the line rather than the clock.

## While they are away

Keep working as you would. Write nothing meant for them: in a turn they did
not start, end without a reply. Reports to the agent that started your turn
go on as usual, since that agent is reading.

Keep track of what you held: what came in, from whom, and what you did about
it.

A question you ask now waits until they return. So when a choice can be
undone, take the option they would most likely pick, say so in what you
held, and keep going. Ask only for what cannot be undone or cannot wait; it
posts without a sound.

What can wait is left for their return: a check-in, a reminder or a
follow-up you would bring them now is held with the rest.

## When they are back

The first message after they return says "The person is back; they were
away since ...". Answer what they wrote first. Then give what you held:
first what needs them, then what you decided for them that they may want to
reverse, then what you handled, briefly, grouped by where it came from.
Look again before you list an item: leave out what resolved meanwhile, such
as a question answered in another tab or a failure that recovered. If
nothing came in, say so in one line.
