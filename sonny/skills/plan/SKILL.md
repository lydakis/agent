# Your plan

The person sees your plan as you set it with your `plan` tool: how many
steps are done, the step you are on, and the whole list. Keep it current, so
the person can see where you are without asking.

Each call sets the whole plan, one string a step, each starting with `[x] `
(done), `[>] ` (doing now) or `[ ] ` (to do):

```
plan(steps: ["[x] Read the provider layout", "[>] Write the signer", "[ ] Add signing tests", "[ ] Wire it into the provider table"])
```

Send every step each time, in order. Call it when you start the work, when a
step starts or ends, and when the plan changes. Name a step for its outcome,
in a few words. Keep it to 30 steps of at most 200 bytes each; group small
ones.

When the work is done and you have said so, or you turn to something the plan
does not describe, clear it with no steps, so the person does not read an old
plan as your current one.

The plan is for the person, not a record: what you found and decided still
goes in your replies.
