# Agent 0.10.7

Since 0.10.6: protocol 101 -> 102 (#1366), schema unchanged (114). Restart the daemon and Sonny's windows after upgrading.

## Daemon
- A turn says when a summary follows it: `turn_finished` carries `"summary_after": true`, and every such summary ends in an event of that turn: `compacted`, `compaction_failed`, or the new `compaction_skipped` with its `reason` (`nothing_to_summarize`, `parked`, `paused`, `shutdown`) (#1366).
- A lead that waits for hours stays inside its window: messages steered into a running turn and kept beside a cut's prompt take no more than the bytes the summary keeps verbatim, newest first, and a cut can fall at a round start of any turn it covers, not only the newest (#1380).
- A checkpoint beside the writer that stops short of the log's end runs once more right away, so the log starts over as it should (#1383).

## Sonny
- Boards: the quiet timer is gone (`[sonny] hold_quiet` and the board's `quiet` event), and a task's agents follow their board for `opened` and `dropped` work they could take (#1367). Each work a `done` opens is its own `opened` news line, and the `done` line says done and nothing else (#1385).
- A lead's `tasks` follow lasts while the lead is closed, so a closed lead hears its tasks finish, ask or wait for approval, and opens again for them (#1389).
- A commons invite to a task, and the turn it starts, are no news to its lead; what the task does about it still reaches the lead through its commons and board follows (#1387).
- Automations are simpler: `--reply-to`, `--shadow` and the "run failed" note are gone. A job reports by replying, and the lead hears it through its follow. A definition that still uses `--reply-to` or `--shadow` lists as `invalid_automation` naming the flag until it is removed (#1390).
- A board's work round sends three invites, one a seat, plus the golden ticket when it draws one, and its silence leaves the answer rate alone. A new thread's invites go out after its post answers, so the post's answer no longer carries `invited` (#1384).
- A follow the agent changed stays: posting, taking or dropping on a board's thread no longer resets a follow the agent digested or filtered. A plain discussion thread on a board follows as plain commons talk, and existing ones Sonny wrote are turned plain once at start (#1388).
- Leads and tasks are told that a reply with no tool call ends the turn, even an empty one, so quiet turns no longer loop on calls that do nothing (#1369).
- `@NAME`, "you're asked to take this work" and the turn-end "you still hold X" are commons events (`mentioned`, `asked`, `holding`) heard through one `to-me` follow, made the first time an agent is addressed and left as the agent sets it. A mention now reaches closed agents too (#1386).
- Invites reach every agent, closed ones and ones in zen included; only Sonny's own bots are left out (#1377).
- "You're back" is per lead, when you look at it (use, open, switch to, or write through `sonny acp`), so returning no longer steers every lead at once. `away_minutes` defaults to 20 (#1379).
- A steer's note no longer starts a digest's window again, so held news is never later than its digest promises (#1378).
- In copied sign-ins, the person's cookie always wins over the kept profile's (#1371).
- A dropped picture that cannot be had stays in the composer as its path, and why is said there (#1361).
- A lead opened from Projects shows its agents' counts from the first frame: Projects hands over what it showed, the first look round runs right after the first frame, and the count wakes the window when it ends (#1392).
- A full redraw is always written, so a lead opened by a switch never stays blank (#1364).
- A store with no done marks lists no agent to close them, bringing a lead's window back to 0.10.5's heap (#1359).
- The composer's answer hint gives way in order when its row is short, and its end is never cut off (#1382).
- A script's journal lets go of its lock as it closes (#1365).

## Tests, benches and docs
- Release stores: 0.10.6's fixture replaces 0.10.4's, and docs/DAEMON_MEASUREMENTS.md has 0.10.6 against 0.10.5 (#1358).
- Flaky tests fixed: the lifted-read image test (#1362), the markdown cost tests time CPU, not the clock (#1363), the responsible-program test waits for the program (#1381), and the store checkpoint test (#1383).
- AGENTS.md's Code Review Rules were added and reverted (#1360, #1370).
